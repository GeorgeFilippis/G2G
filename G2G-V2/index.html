Model 11 scores each place as need × conversion value × a people-nearby factor. A 10 m square is left out only when buildings cover more than half of it; squares that touch a road or a building are kept and marked for an on-site check. Where no heat model value exists, heat is estimated and never given as the main reason. All maps use one colour scale (green = 0, yellow = 0.375, dark red = 0.75 or more), so the same score has the same colour at 10 m and at 1 m. At 1 m the pixels are ranked within their own neighbourhood; in the part of Port North where heat is estimated, the map stays at 10 m.
