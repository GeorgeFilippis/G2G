Model 11 scores each place as need × conversion value × a people-nearby factor. Uppsala uses Malmö's rules and Malmö's weights, with Uppsala data: MSB's land-surface temperature for heat, Uppsala kommun's 2022 tree-canopy map, NMD 2023 land cover, Uppsala's detailed plans for zoning, and SCB statistics for people and income. Heat vulnerability has weight 0, because Uppsala has no index like Malmö's. A 10 m square is left out only when buildings cover more than half of it; squares that touch a road or a building are kept and marked for an on-site check. All maps use the same colour scale as the Malmö maps (green = 0, yellow = 0.375, dark red = 0.75 or more), so the same score has the same colour in both cities, at 10 m and at 1 m.

The 1 m test square. Uppsala has no land-cover map finer than 10 m, so for Kapellgärdet the ground at 0.25 m was drawn from map layers (buildings, roads, paths, parking, lawns, tree canopy). About a quarter of the square is ground none of them covers; there the 10 m sealed share was placed next to mapped paving, and the popup says when a pixel is such inferred ground. At 1 m, pixels are ranked within the square.
